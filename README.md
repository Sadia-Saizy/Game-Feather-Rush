# 🦆 Feather Rush: Peep, Chirp & Quack! ✨

**Feather Rush** is a fast-paced, browser-based HTML5 side-scrolling runner game. 

This game was built with love for my daughter, who absolutely adores birds! Every single character, concept, power-up, and game mechanic in *Feather Rush* came straight from her wonderful imagination.

## 🌟 Game Features

* **Beloved Characters:** Play as **Quack** (the purple duck in his sailor hat), joined by **Peep** and **Wisdom Bird** as they race across the field to reach **Chirp**!
* **Creative Power-Ups:** 
  * **Super Seed:** Quack grows giant and smashes through obstacles!
  * **Three Wings:** Grants extra high jumps and floating ability.
  * **Guard Bird:** Protects Quack with an energetic aura shield.
  * **Rich Box:** Boosts running speed across the field.
  * **Money Tape & Santa Bird:** Clears incoming obstacles out of the way.
* **Duck Wisdom Stash:** Collect shiny acorns to build up your score, with personal high scores saved locally in your browser.
* **Web Audio API Sound Effects:** Custom synthesized jump sounds, acorn chimes, and game-over tones generated entirely in code—no external audio files needed!

## 🎮 How to Play

Choose how you want to play:
1. Open the live game link on GitHub Pages.
2. Click **START GAME ▶**.
3. Use the **HIGH JUMP** button, **Spacebar**, or **Up Arrow** to jump over obstacles and collect acorns.
4. Press **R** to restart when game over!
